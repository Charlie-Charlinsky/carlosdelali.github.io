Echoes of Blood

Distant voices laden with nauseating fatigue evoke the desperation of fleeing this place, whose return is still far from present. The sound of despair reverberates through the rocky cavities, guiding my doom with a steady step toward the accursed resonance which, along the grim path, seems to offer hope of an end to this gloomy and solitary march. With every step, the voices turn into screams, the nausea into guttural gasps, all accompanied by the presence of a gelatinous sound that seems to spread through the stony ventricle.

At the end of the cavity, a light takes shape. As I struggle between the suffocating stone walls to reach the glow at the end of the tunnel, a rockfall ruins my entrance into the gigantic excavated hiding place. The rush of my companions is drowned beneath the rain of stone, giving way to an intense silence that seems to lend palpable density to the embrace of this opaque darkness. The diodes on my caving suit activate when they detect the absence of their own essence, illuminating the surroundings with a warm orange light. Through my communicator, some guards inform me that my position has been detected and that a rescue agent is on the way.

Meanwhile, the fearful curiosity invading me tempts me to place my hands upon the rock wall, which I set about dismantling with the excavation tools equipped on my suit. During the deconstruction process, an intense viscous sound envelops the entire rocky artery. Through the holes and fissures opened in the formed wall, a thick reddish fluid slides down onto my suit. From this sticky biomass spat through the openings in the wall, extensions begin to emerge, intertwining with the liquid spilled over my figure and forming a glutinous reddish web around me.

The viscosity slides along the cavern walls, trapping me within its pattern of strands and rendering useless any effort to escape this oppressive tangle made of long reddish capillaries. After I have almost become a chrysalis, the strands inside it begin to invade my skin, burrowing through the flesh until they connect with my bloodstream. My consciousness ignites, surpassing the unprecedented limits that even my suspicion had been forbidden to probe; all of it to preserve what little sanity this underworld has drained from me by forcing me to face it.

The immensity of the unknown invades the order to which my sanity can no longer cling. The infinity granted by dissolution is comprehensible for only a breath of time, soothing my agitated soul; an anaesthetic against the horror of this process, against the fear of being nobody and everything at once, our existence now reaffirmed as a single living entity. In transit, through an intense orange light produced by the hallucinations, I can hear hundreds of thousands of voices: the voices of infinity, all of them whispering, screaming, crying, speaking and even lamenting; a sea of sound to which my voice will be entrusted so that this requiem may endure, one that only those fortunate enough to abandon the dark lament evoked by the reality to which we have been subjected will be able to contemplate in this process of collective union.

The ecstasy caused by this strange experience makes me awaken embraced by a tidal wave of spasms inside the incubator. The blood-renewal systems withdraw the probes connected throughout my body, leaving the interior of my circulatory system. The hatches of this coffin of forced rest open slowly, welcoming me back to my habitation cubicle. This cold shelter, plated in dark metal across all its walls, lowers the daily-task monitor. On it, a digital map displays the position I must reach in the mine.

The cubicle descends slowly. Above the exit doors, a screen shows the identifier of the mine entrance where I am located, until it stops at the exit marked with the characters: six and ‘F’. The doors open, allowing me into the preparation room, where an armoured carbon-fibre suit and a binary-light rifle are laid out for me before I enter a new tale laden with doom.

The depressurisation hatch welcomes me once again into the unknown: a strange atmosphere filled with reddish particles invades the chamber. As I travel through the mined ventricles, the visual noise created by thousands of these particles clinging to the glass of my helmet increases the feeling of perdition along the opaque path. After passing several supply facilities, I reach a timid entrance, no larger than a door, set precisely at the centre of one of the rock walls. From within that same opening, hundreds of reddish roots emerge outward, gripping the stone walls.

After entering through the vaginal opening, the walls begin to reveal an interwoven fabric of reddish capillaries, within which apparent faces seem to take shape, dissolved across the tangle of strands. The path narrows, strangling my torso and breaking the capillaries with the violent friction of my body, plunging the walls of this tight passage into a solitary bloody weeping: where the visceral echo of entrails tearing free imposes itself as a lament within this clamour for death.

A few metres away, inside a broad recess, an intense orange light can be distinguished. Every step taken through this grotto summons images of the dream produced a few hours earlier in the incubator. My perception of reality collapses; I feel as though I have already travelled this path on several occasions. Upon reaching the great recess, I lose all notion of what is real when I see that the wall where a strange orb of light, built upon a base of capillaries on the ground, stands before the enormous rock face in which, at some point in my existence, I was devoured by this biomass of reddish roots. Everything becomes as real as the dissolution to which I was subjected only a few hours ago.
