Synapse

A damp autumn night casts its presence over a town raised atop a mountain range, where dozens of hurried inhabitants slip through the winding streets that form the two hemispheres of this district. The lights of the timid flames resting upon the torches of these faithful finally gather before a long road that climbs toward the outskirts of the town and disappears through the entrance to the gorges of Rienvel.

Along the broad Rúa de Los Penitentes, a procession is carried out by a group of parishioners whose faces and bodies are covered by long, striking crimson robes. The congregation carries an entirely empty glass coffin with steady steps, while a long line of believers follows them up the street on their knees, allowing their skin to tear with every deep impact against the pronounced cobblestones of the path.

The anxious spectators are left in awe by the performance of these faithful, inflaming their spirit, allowing fanaticism to possess what little sanity this place grants them, giving themselves permission to take their own lives with rusty daggers whose almost absent edge tests their faithful will for sacrifice. Others prefer to sacrifice their own sons and daughters, sparing these innocents a calamitous existence in this place built by the very hand of despair.

Amid the torrent of water and blood flowing between the feet and knees of those chosen for the ceremony, the way out appears before them completely sealed. A gigantic pointed stone arch embraces a sealed metal gate upon which shine images of passages described by texts in a strange language, still unknown to those who have not ventured into the hidden.
