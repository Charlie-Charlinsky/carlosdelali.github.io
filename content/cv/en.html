EA Sports — Madrid, Spain

Senior Game Designer | Jul. 2021 – Jul. 2026

  • My experience at EA Sports is defined by four projects I developed at the Madrid studio: EA SPORTS PGA TOUR: Road to the Masters and Superstar for Madden NFL 25, 26, and 27. These projects shared one key direction: making the player's sports fantasy as unique and immersive as possible through narrative systems and RPG progression.
  • My first three years of development focused on PGA TOUR and its launch:
    • The Madrid design team consisted of just two designers: my Lead and me, with responsibility for the game's entire design scope. The project was developed fully remotely over three years.
    • My responsibilities as a designer included end-to-end ownership of the progression system; XP and in-game economy balancing using custom formulas; development of Quest, Challenge, Tournament, and Live Battle Pass systems; and all the content supporting each of these systems.
    • Expanded the golfer's RPG skill system, connecting progression choices to the builds players could create throughout their careers.
    • Designed gameplay systems and UI for ball striking, defining how the new shot interactions worked and how their states and feedback were communicated to the player.
    • Supported the game throughout Live Services by creating recurring seasons through the Battle Pass system I developed.
  • During my final year on PGA TOUR, I collaborated on Superstar for Madden NFL 25 for nearly its entire production cycle:
    • Worked on the progression systems through launch. These systems enabled players to create customised builds through their choice of archetype.
    • Helped oversee the XP economy system, which allowed players not only to level up their characters, but also to unlock new abilities within their chosen archetype using currencies such as Skill Points.
  • On Superstar for Madden NFL 26, I contributed throughout the full cycle, from pre-production through the entire Live Services period:
    • Designed Sphere of Influence: an RPG system whose main purpose was to forge relationships with every character encountered throughout the player's career, shaped by the decisions and actions taken across their story. This enabled replayability, unlocked new rewards through each character's trust level within the player's sphere of influence, and determined the narrative path available based on how those decisions affected each character.
    • After launch, I spent the entire Live Services period developing events for Superstar Showdown and their reward systems, while expanding the character-level system through the release of the next title.
  • Superstar for Madden NFL 27 introduced significant changes to several existing gameplay systems, alongside new systems designed to create a clear difference from the previous title:
    • Battle Pass: A system that allowed me to create a complete content package across twelve seasons distributed throughout the year. It centralised the content from every gameplay system and the connections between them. Its key features included premium and free Battle Pass tracks and rewards; Quests and new missions; new Showdown events; new Sphere of Influence characters; and more.
    • Sphere of Influence: A major update introducing new features with a deeper impact on the characters within the player's circle and the different narrative paths available to them.
    • Side Activities: A purely narrative dialogue-choice system. It allowed players to determine which characters were affected by each response, potentially strengthening or damaging relationships, unlocking new ones, or losing them entirely. This content was postponed to Superstar for Madden NFL 28.
  • Across all four projects, I followed a similar development process to bring each of my designs to life:
    • Built scalable pipelines for each system and its content using Frostbite and Perforce, SQL, Excel, custom tools, and structured GDDs that made the systems easier to understand and supported collaboration across production, engineering, and QV.
  • Selected Titles: EA SPORTS PGA TOUR: Road to the Masters and Madden NFL 26.
