Carlos J. L. Sánchez

Game Designer

About

Carlos is a generalist game designer focused on experimenting with new forms of entertainment through systemic design. After 10 years in the industry, he has worked across a wide range of projects, from F2P mobile and casino games to AAA titles for console and PC.

At Genera Games and TWS, he worked across a range of mobile and casino projects. As the sole designer across the projects he took on, he had the opportunity to grow across nearly every area of game design: progression, balancing and economy systems, level design, prototyping, rules design, and innovation within the technical and conceptual constraints of casino games. This can be seen in titles such as Gold Rush Gus, where casino and F2P mechanics come together to create a distinctive hybrid experience.

As his career matured at EA SPORTS, he worked on AAA productions where the systems were built around a strong player fantasy: allowing players to shape their own experience through a professional sports career. RPG progression and narrative systems were especially important in PGA TOUR and Madden NFL 26, where his work covered progression and abilities, balancing, quests, challenges and tournaments, as well as narrative systems such as Sphere of Influence, including the creation of the content that powered these systems.

Alongside his professional work, Carlos maintains an independent creative interest in worldbuilding from an introspective perspective. His process begins with finding a personal idea that can be transformed into an experience capable of creating a connection between himself and the player on the other side of the screen.
