Carlos J. L. Sánchez

Game Designer

About

I am a generalist game designer focused on experimenting with new forms of entertainment through systemic design. After 10 years in the industry, I have worked across a range of projects, from F2P mobile and casino games to AAA titles for console and PC.

At Genera Games and TWS, I began my career developing a range of mobile and casino games. As the sole designer across every project I took on, I had the opportunity to develop my skills across every area and system of game design: from progression, balancing and economy systems to level design, prototyping, rules design, and innovation within the technical and conceptual constraints of casino games. This can be seen in standout titles such as Gold Rush Gus, where casino and F2P come together to create a unique experience.

As my career matured at EA SPORTS, I worked on AAA productions where systems were designed to serve a strong sports fantasy: enabling players to create their own experience through a professional sports career. RPG progression and narrative systems were particularly important in PGA TOUR and Madden NFL 26, where I worked across progression and abilities, balancing, quests, challenges and tournaments, as well as narrative systems such as Sphere of Influence, including the creation of the content that supported all these systems.

In my independent creative work, I maintain an interest in worldbuilding. My process begins by finding an idea rooted in personal experience that can be transformed into an experience capable of creating a connection between me and the player.
