About

I am a Game Designer with more than ten years of experience across AAA sports, mobile and casino games. My work has moved between systems design, gameplay progression, player abilities, reward structures, level design and technical prototyping, always with the same objective: turning an idea into rules that can be understood, tested, challenged and eventually played.

I tend to approach design systemically. I am interested in the relationship between what a player wants to do, what the game allows them to do, how the system reacts, and how those decisions accumulate into progression, mastery or consequence. That way of thinking has led me to work with data-driven systems, probability models, gameplay-facing mechanics, narrative-linked choices and scalable content pipelines across Frostbite, Unreal Engine and Unity.

The part of design I value most is the process itself: questioning assumptions, researching behaviour, drawing structures on a whiteboard, prototyping quickly and iterating with people from different disciplines until the idea becomes coherent. I enjoy projects that sit between creative intuition and technical constraint, especially when they force me to understand a problem from several angles rather than solve it with a single mechanic.

Outside game development, I write short stories, compose music, study science and innovation, and keep a dream journal. Those interests are not separate from how I design; they are different ways of observing systems, behaviour, imagery and ideas, and they continuously feed the kind of games and projects I want to build.
