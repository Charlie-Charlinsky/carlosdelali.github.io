The Game

Gold Rush Gus was designed with the purpose of exploring innovation within the casino slot space.

The idea was to combine the classic mechanics of slot games with mechanics from F2P mobile games, creating a new way of experiencing casino games. Narrative was added to the game as another layer, strengthening both its artistic direction and the depth of the experience itself.

The game follows a miner whose main goal is to find the great gold nugget. Through each spin, the player moves through a mine where Gus, the main character, collects valuable treasures. Across an adventure filled with treasure hunting, mine-cart races and encounters with an antagonist who wants to steal the great nugget, players experience a slot game that moves away from the artificial feel of a traditional slot machine, creating a more organic and stimulating narrative-driven experience.
