Overview

Gold Rush Gus was a mining-themed slot built around 5 reels and 10 paylines, with feature layers including Four Rewards, Free Spins, Lava Re-spin and mining-themed bonus/jackpot presentation. I owned the game design at TWS Inventors of Play, defining the experience and guiding it from concept through documentation, mathematical validation, prototyping and production support.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the game's GDDs and behaviour documentation, specifying the base-game rules, feature triggers, state transitions, reward logic, edge cases and implementation requirements. That documentation gave mathematicians, engineers and artists a shared reference for how every system and presentation state should behave.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I developed the feature behaviour iteratively on paper and whiteboard, then worked with mathematicians to test the probability model and balance implications. Unity/C# prototypes and simulations helped us validate frequencies and payout behaviour. The design work focused on how Four Rewards, Free Spins and Lava Re-spin modified the base spin loop, how players entered and exited those states, and how the mining-themed bonus presentation communicated the result.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked directly with the mathematics, engineering and art disciplines throughout production, resolving design questions as the game moved from documentation into implementation. This let me keep the mechanical rules, mathematical behaviour and visual presentation coherent as a single experience.
