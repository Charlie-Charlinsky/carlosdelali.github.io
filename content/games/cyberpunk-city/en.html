Overview

Cyberpunk City was an anime/cyberpunk-themed slot built around 5 reels and 20 paylines. Its feature set combined Expanding Wilds, Random Wilds, multipliers and a Random Progressive Jackpot, creating a more layered interaction between base spins, feature states and jackpot presentation. I was the design owner for the game at TWS Inventors of Play.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I authored the GDDs and behaviour specifications that defined the game's rules from end to end: symbol behaviour, feature triggers, state transitions, jackpot logic, edge cases, UI feedback and implementation requirements. The documentation was the shared reference used to align design intent with engineering and art production.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I iterated on the feature architecture through whiteboard exploration, then worked with mathematicians to test whether the combined mechanics were mathematically viable and balanceable. Unity/C# prototyping and simulation allowed us to validate hit frequencies, payout behaviour and interactions between Expanding Wilds, Random Wilds, multipliers and the progressive jackpot before the rules were locked for production.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists as the game moved from design into production. My role was to keep the feature behaviour clear and implementable while making sure the mathematical model, technical implementation and visual feedback all supported the same intended player experience.
