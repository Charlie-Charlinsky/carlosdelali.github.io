The Game

Cyberpunk City was created at a time when the cyberpunk aesthetic was gaining momentum and Cyberpunk 2077 was generating enormous anticipation. The intention was to bring that dark, technological and violent lore into a slot machine without leaving the theme as a purely visual layer. Each payline was associated with a combat action performed by the main character, so a winning combination also had a representation within the action taking place on screen.

The game uses a 5×3 layout with 20 paylines. Players enter a dystopian city accompanied by a sidekick, whose selection forms part of the identity of the session. On top of that structure, cyberpunk characters and symbols are mixed with different Wild behaviours so that spins have a more dynamic reading than those of a traditional slot.

Its main mechanics include Expanding Wilds, Random Wilds, multipliers and a Random Progressive Jackpot. These features can change the composition of the reels, expand winning combinations and scale the result of a spin, reinforcing the sense of conflict, risk and reward that defines the overall tone of the game.
