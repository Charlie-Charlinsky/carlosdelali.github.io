The Game

Zombie Soccer was created as a re-adaptation of the structure we had used in Cricket Legends. The idea was to preserve a game system that already worked and completely change its identity, mixing football and zombies to build a more grotesque, exaggerated and humorous version of the same type of experience.

The version released as Zombie FC keeps a 5×3 layout with 243 ways to win and uses Tumbling Reels so that a single spin can generate several consecutive combinations. Wilds can appear stacked or expand to occupy an entire reel, while players, referees and sporting environments are transformed into characters and locations from a stadium after the apocalypse.

Landing three, four or five Trophy Scatters triggers 15, 20 or 25 Free Spins respectively. During the feature, consecutive wins increase a progressive multiplier up to x10. In this way, the game preserves much of the mechanical structure of Cricket Legends and moves it into a completely different visual identity.
