Overview

Zombie Soccer was a football-themed zombie slot project developed at TWS Inventors of Play. The game combined a multi-way reel structure with bonus/reward states and a presentation built around the collision of sports and zombie themes. I was the design owner for the project and defined its behaviour from concept through production documentation.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and behaviour specifications for the game, covering reel and win logic, feature triggers, reward states, transitions, edge cases, UI feedback and implementation requirements. The documents were the central reference for translating the design into mathematics, code and art.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I explored the feature logic on paper and whiteboard, then worked with mathematicians to validate the probability model, payout behaviour and balance. Unity/C# prototyping and simulation were used to test the game flow before production rules were locked. The design work focused on keeping the core reel behaviour, bonus/reward states and win presentation readable while supporting the more expressive sports/zombie theme.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I collaborated closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists throughout development, reviewing the playable implementation against the design documents and resolving changes across rules, balance, technical behaviour and visual feedback. That ownership helped keep the game coherent as different disciplines contributed to the final experience.
