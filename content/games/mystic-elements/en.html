The Game

Mystic Elements was one of the most personal projects I developed at TWS, and its original concept came entirely from an idea of my own. I wanted to use the alchemical elements as the thematic foundation, but move them away from a purely esoteric representation and connect them to forces found in nature: earth, air, water, fire, ether and light. The intention was for that symbolism to have a direct function within the rules of the game.

The game uses a 5×3 layout with 10 paylines. There is no traditional Wild: when one or two Ether Symbols appear, they can transform into other symbols to create new winning combinations. If three or more appear, they become Free Spins symbols and trigger the bonus round.

Three, four or five Free Spins symbols award 10, 15 or 20 spins respectively, with retriggers available up to a maximum total of 150. During the feature, wins receive a x3 multiplier and the Light Symbol becomes active; landing five Light Symbols can trigger the Progressive Jackpot. In this way, the natural elements themselves become a direct part of the game's reward progression.
