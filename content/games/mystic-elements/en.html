Overview

Mystic Elements was an original slot concept I created at TWS Inventors of Play. Built around six elemental forces — earth, air, water, fire, ether and light — the game combined a 5-reel, 10-payline base with Mystery Symbol transformation, free spins and a progressive-jackpot layer. I owned the design from the initial concept through documentation, mathematical validation, prototyping and implementation support.

My Contribution

Original Concept, Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the original concept and defined the game's behaviour in its GDDs and implementation-facing specifications. This included the base loop, symbol behaviours, trigger conditions, feature states, transitions, edge cases and the relationship between the six elemental themes and the slot mechanics. The documentation acted as the source of truth for design, engineering, mathematics and art.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I explored the mechanics on paper and whiteboard before working closely with mathematicians to test feasibility and shape the probability model. Unity/C# prototypes and simulations were used to validate frequencies, payout behaviour and balance. The core design centred on the Ether/Mystery Symbol transforming into other symbols, free-spin progression from multiple trigger counts, retriggers, and the Light-symbol condition connected to the progressive jackpot.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists throughout development, reviewing the implementation against the GDD and iterating when the mathematical model, technical constraints or visual communication required changes. That collaboration allowed the original concept to move from an abstract elemental idea into a playable, testable production game.
