Overview

Xtreme Racing 2 was a racing game built around three vehicle categories: RC cars, speed boats and 4x4 RC cars. During the project, changes to the physics and controller behaviour required a broad rebalance of the experience. My work focused on the vehicle-improvement system, level and tutorial redesign, race difficulty and FX.

My Contribution

Vehicle Progression & Store

I worked on the car-improvement section of the game, where vehicle upgrades shaped progression and prepared the player for increasingly demanding races. I rebalanced those improvements to reshape the difficulty curve and implemented the required values through an Excel format that could be imported into Unity.

The work connected upgrade progression with the challenge expected from the races, so changes to vehicle growth and race difficulty could be tuned as part of the same balancing problem.

Race & Level Design

The physics upgrade changed how the vehicles behaved, so existing races also needed to be rebalanced. I redesigned game levels and tutorials and adjusted the difficulty of the cars the player had to defeat, iterating on the races around the revised controller behaviour.

FX & Polish

As part of the polishing work, I introduced new FX systems to support the updated gameplay and presentation.
