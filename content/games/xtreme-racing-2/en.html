The Game

Xtreme Racing 2 is a series of arcade racing games featuring radio-controlled vehicles. The project was built around three disciplines: high-speed RC cars, speedboats and RC 4x4s. Races combine driving, jumps, obstacles, collisions with rivals and boost zones, creating an immediate experience far removed from the realism of a traditional simulator.

Progression is built around the garage and vehicle upgrades. Winning races unlocks more demanding events, while allowing players to improve vehicle stats and customise their ride with new parts and visual elements. As a result, track and opponent difficulty is directly tied to vehicle growth and the player’s upgrade choices.

Contribution

The Shop

The project received a major update to its physics and vehicle-controller behaviour, requiring a complete rebalance. I focused on programming and retuning the vehicle upgrade section to rebuild the progression curve. Each upgrade value was managed in a specially formatted Excel file that could be imported directly into Unity, allowing us to iterate on speed, acceleration, handling and other parameters without manually editing data in the engine.

The Races

The physics changes also affected the difficulty of existing races. I redesigned levels and tutorials, retuned the AI-controlled vehicles and revalidated the progression pacing across events. As part of the final polish, I also implemented new FX systems to reinforce the sense of speed, impacts, boosts and standout moments during competition.
