Overview

For Madden NFL 25, I worked on Superstar archetypes, player progression and player abilities through release. The design challenge was to make each build feel distinct while keeping growth, costs and unlock pacing coherent across the career experience.

My work connected archetype identity with attribute development and ability access, supporting a progression structure in which player choices shaped the strengths and limitations of each Superstar build.

My Contribution

Archetypes & Build Identity

I worked on the systems that connected Superstar archetypes to the attributes and progression rules that defined each build. The objective was to preserve a clear gameplay identity for each archetype while ensuring that progression remained understandable and balanced across the career.

This meant treating archetype identity as a systemic constraint: attribute ceilings, growth opportunities and later ability access all had to reinforce the intended strengths of the build.

Progression & Attributes

I supported the progression structure that governed how attributes developed over time, including costs, pacing and the relationship between player investment and overall growth. The system needed to provide meaningful advancement without allowing progression to flatten the differences between archetypes.

Balancing therefore focused on the interaction between progression pace, attribute limits and the long-term shape of the player build.

Player Abilities

I worked on the ability layer connected to Superstar progression, including the rules that determined when abilities became available and how they related to the player's developing attributes and archetype.

The goal was for ability unlocks to act as meaningful milestones within the progression loop rather than isolated rewards, reinforcing the identity and gameplay direction of the build.

Data-Driven Implementation & Validation

The work was implemented and validated through data-driven systems, allowing progression values, requirements and ability relationships to be iterated as the feature moved toward release.

I worked across design, engineering, production and QV to test assumptions, resolve issues and keep the authored progression logic aligned with the shipped player experience.
