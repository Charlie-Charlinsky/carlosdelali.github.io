The Game

In Madden NFL 25 Superstar, the core design challenge was to give every build a clear identity while keeping growth, costs, and unlock pacing coherent throughout the career.

My work connected archetype identity with attribute development and access to abilities, supporting a progression structure in which player choices defined the strengths and limitations of each Superstar build.

Contribution

Archetypes & Build Identity

I worked on the systems connecting Superstar archetypes to the attributes and progression rules that defined each build. The goal was to preserve a clear gameplay identity for every archetype while ensuring progression remained understandable and balanced throughout the career.

This meant treating the chosen archetype as a systemic constraint: attribute caps, growth opportunities, and later access to abilities all had to reinforce the build's intended strengths.

Progression & Attributes

I supported the progression architecture governing how attributes evolved over time, including costs, frequency, and the relationship between player investment and overall growth. The system needed to deliver meaningful progression without allowing growth to erase the differences between archetypes.

Balancing therefore focused on the interaction between progression pacing, attribute caps, and the long-term shape of the player's build.

Player Abilities

I worked on the ability layer connected to Superstar progression, including the rules that determined when abilities unlocked and how they related to developing attributes and the player's archetype.

The goal was for ability unlocks to function as meaningful milestones within the progression loop rather than isolated rewards, reinforcing the build's identity and gameplay direction.

Data-Driven Implementation & Validation

The work was implemented and validated through data-driven systems, allowing us to iterate on progression values, requirements, and relationships between abilities as the feature moved toward launch.

I collaborated with design, engineering, production, and QV to test hypotheses, resolve issues, and keep the progression logic aligned with the intended player experience.
