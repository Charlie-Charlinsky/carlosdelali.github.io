The Game

Andar Bahar was conceived as a fresh digital adaptation of a traditional Indian card game. The intention was to preserve an extremely simple central rule and support it with a fast, clear interface that worked comfortably on mobile, allowing players to understand the game almost immediately and move through rounds without unnecessary interruption.

Each round begins with a central reference card. The player bets on Andar or Bahar, and cards are then dealt alternately to both sides until a card matching the rank of the central card appears. The side that receives that match determines the result of the round.

Five side bets were added around this core rule to expand the available betting options, together with Re-bet and Re-bet x2 controls for quickly repeating or doubling the previous wager. Players can also customise the background and use a full-screen mobile presentation, keeping the overall experience lightweight, direct and easy to read.
