Overview

Andar Bahar was a digital table-game project built around the central choice of whether the matching card would resolve on the Andar or Bahar side, with additional betting options layered onto a fast round loop. At TWS Inventors of Play, I owned the design and was responsible for defining the game behaviour from rules through implementation documentation.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and behaviour specifications for the complete round flow: bet placement, state transitions, card dealing and reveal, outcome resolution, payout logic, optional betting behaviours, edge cases and UI feedback. This documentation was the common reference used by mathematics, engineering and art during production.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I worked with mathematicians to validate the probability model, payout relationships and the impact of the additional betting options. Simulation and Unity/C# prototyping were used to test the flow and balance before implementation was finalised. The design challenge was to preserve the immediacy of Andar Bahar while making the digital states and betting feedback unambiguous.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I collaborated directly with mathematicians, engineers and artists throughout development, clarifying rules and reviewing the implemented behaviour against the design documents. This allowed the mathematical model, technical flow and table presentation to evolve together rather than as disconnected production tasks.
