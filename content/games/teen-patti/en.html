The Game

Teen Patti was approached as a fresh digital adaptation of one of India's most popular casino card games. The intention was to preserve a structure that its regular audience could recognise while translating it into a cleaner, faster and more direct experience, designed so that each round could be understood and resolved with very little friction.

The public version is structured around three main outcomes: Player, Dealer or Split. Players bet on which of the two hands will win, or on a split result. From that main wager, they can also predict the category of the winning hand through a set of additional side bets.

Re-bet and Re-bet x2 make it possible to repeat or double the previous wager quickly, reducing the number of steps between rounds and keeping the pace very fast. The result is an adaptation that preserves the identity of Teen Patti while presenting it through an interface and flow better suited to browsers and mobile devices.
