Overview

Teen Patti was a digital table-game project based on the traditional three-card casino format. My work at TWS Inventors of Play focused on translating a compact physical betting game into a precise digital round flow, with clearly defined rules, states, outcomes and player feedback. I was the design owner for the project.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and behaviour documentation used by the team, defining the round structure, betting states, dealing and reveal flow, outcome resolution, payout behaviour, edge cases and UI requirements. The goal was to make every state explicit enough that engineering, mathematics and art could implement the same game behaviour without ambiguity.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I worked closely with mathematicians to review hand probabilities, payout relationships and the feasibility of the betting structure, using simulation and Unity/C# prototyping to validate the model before production decisions were locked. The design process focused on making the table-game flow readable and deterministic while preserving the underlying probability-driven experience.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I coordinated continuously with mathematicians, engineers and artists as the rules moved from documentation into the playable game. I reviewed implementation questions, clarified behaviour and iterated on presentation so the mathematical outcome, technical state machine and player-facing feedback remained aligned.
