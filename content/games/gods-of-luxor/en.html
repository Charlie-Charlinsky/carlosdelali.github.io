The Game

Gods Of Luxor started from a very direct idea: explore the imagery of Egyptian mythology and build the game around a recursive spinning system. The goal was for a win to open new reward opportunities through multipliers, Free Spins and additional spins, creating a sense of continuity within the loop of a classic slot.

The game uses a 5×3 layout with 20 paylines and a collection of symbols inspired by the gods and iconography of Ancient Egypt. The Scarab acts as the Wild, replacing other symbols and doubling the combinations it contributes to, while the Ankh works as the Scatter and provides access to the Free Spins round.

Landing three or more Ankhs triggers 15 Free Spins, during which winnings receive a x3 multiplier. The round can be retriggered up to a maximum total of 180 spins. After certain winning spins, the Wheel of Gods can also appear, applying a multiplier to the previous prize and extending the sense of reward beyond the initial result.
