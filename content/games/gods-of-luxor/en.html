Overview

Gods of Luxor was an Egyptian-themed slot project built around a 5-reel, 20-payline structure, with feature layers including Free Spins and Win and Spin. At TWS Inventors of Play, I owned the game design and defined the rules, documentation and behaviour that the team used to take the project from concept into implementation.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and implementation-facing behaviour specifications for the project, covering base-game rules, feature activation, state transitions, reward resolution, edge cases and UI/animation requirements. This gave the wider team a precise source of truth for how the game should behave across every major state.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I explored the mechanics through paper and whiteboard iteration, then worked with mathematicians to validate the probability model and balance. Unity/C# prototypes and simulations were used to test frequencies and payout behaviour. The design work focused on the relationship between the base game, Free Spins, Win and Spin and the presentation of high-value outcomes.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I collaborated closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists throughout the project, reviewing implementation against the GDDs and resolving changes caused by mathematical findings, technical constraints or presentation needs. That collaboration let the design evolve without losing consistency between rules, balance and player feedback.
