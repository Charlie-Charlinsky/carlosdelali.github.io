Overview

777 Deluxe was a classic slot built around a 5-reel, 10-payline structure, with Wild and Scatter behaviour, Mystery Symbols, free spins and bonus/jackpot layers. At TWS Inventors of Play, I was the design owner for the game and carried the design from concept definition through documentation, prototyping, mathematical validation and implementation support.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and behaviour specifications used by the team, defining the core loop, symbol rules, trigger conditions, feature states, transitions, edge cases and implementation-facing requirements. The documentation acted as the source of truth for how the game should behave across design, engineering and art.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I explored the mechanics on paper and whiteboard, then worked closely with mathematicians to test feasibility and shape the probability model. Unity/C# prototypes and simulations were used to validate frequencies, payout behaviour and balance. For 777 Deluxe, the design work focused on how Mystery Symbols, Wilds, Scatters, free-spin triggers and bonus/jackpot presentation interacted with the base game.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists throughout development. We reviewed the game against the design documentation, iterating whenever the mathematical model, technical constraints or visual feedback required adjustments so the implemented behaviour remained aligned with the intended experience.
