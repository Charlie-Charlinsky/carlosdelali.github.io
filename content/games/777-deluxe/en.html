The Game

777 Deluxe was designed as a reinterpretation of the classic slot machine without losing the simplicity that makes this type of game immediately recognisable. The idea was to preserve the symbols and behaviours of traditional machines while introducing a more modern visual style and a set of mechanics capable of creating more variety within the main loop. The goal was for players to understand the game quickly while still finding small changes and rewards that broke up the repetition of regular spins.

The game uses a 5×3 layout with 10 paylines. To win, players need to line up three or more matching symbols, with combinations paying from left to right, right to left, or through specific combinations across the three central reels. The paytable mixes classic casino symbols such as fruit, bells, horseshoes, BAR symbols and sevens with special symbols that change the normal behaviour of the reels.

One of the main elements is the Mystery Symbol, which can transform into other symbols to complete or improve winning combinations. Landing three Mystery Symbols at the same time turns them into Bonus Symbols and triggers a small three-reel, single-payline bonus game with prizes of up to x250. The current version available on Bovada also includes the Hot Drop Jackpots system, where three Jackpot Symbols open a prize wheel offering Hourly, Daily and Epic awards.
