The Game

Cricket Legends was developed for a client in the Indian market who wanted a game capable of tapping into the enormous cultural presence of cricket among its audience. The intention was for the sport to be more than a visual layer: the characters, stadium, symbols and rhythm of the mechanics all had to convey the feeling of being inside a match.

The game uses a 5×3 layout with 243 ways to win, allowing combinations to form across adjacent reels without relying on traditional paylines. After a winning combination, the symbols disappear and new ones tumble down from above through the Tumbling Reels mechanic. Stacked Wilds and Expanding Wilds add further opportunities to create new wins within the same spin.

Landing three, four or five Trophy Scatters triggers 15, 20 or 25 Free Spins respectively. During the feature, consecutive wins progressively increase the multiplier up to x10, allowing a strong chain of results to grow significantly before the multiplier resets.
