Overview

Cricket Legends was a cricket-themed slot built around 5 reels and 243 ways, with feature layers including Stacked Wilds, Bonus Games, free-spin/multiplier states and dedicated big-win presentation. At TWS Inventors of Play, I was the design owner and defined the game from concept and rules through documentation, mathematical validation and implementation support.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I authored the GDDs and behaviour specifications used by the team, documenting the reel and win rules, feature triggers, state transitions, reward logic, edge cases, UI feedback and implementation requirements. The documentation gave mathematics, engineering and art a shared definition of how the game should behave.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I developed the feature set iteratively through paper and whiteboard exploration, then worked with mathematicians to validate hit frequency, payout behaviour and balance. Unity/C# prototypes and simulation helped test the 243-ways structure, Stacked Wild behaviour, bonus-game triggers and free-spin/multiplier states before the rules were finalised for production.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists as the game moved into implementation, reviewing technical and visual results against the design documents and adjusting the specification where necessary. The goal was to keep the probability model, game flow and sports-themed presentation coherent from the same design intent.
