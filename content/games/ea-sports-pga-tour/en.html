Overview

EA SPORTS PGA TOUR was the project where my systems design work expanded into a full AAA production cycle. I worked on the game for three years in a fully remote environment, with a high degree of autonomy across progression, content pipelines, live-service systems and player-facing gameplay features.

My contribution connected the golfer's long-term growth with repeatable content systems: quests and challenges, tournaments, Event Pass, XP and level progression, Skill Point investment, ability unlocks, and the gameplay/UI layer around ball striking.

My Contribution

Quests & Challenges

I helped deliver the system used to design and implement different types of quests and challenges. The workflow connected design, implementation and testing through a data-driven pipeline built around SQL, Excel and custom tools, enabling consistent authoring and validation before release.

The design challenge was not simply to create individual objectives, but to build a reusable structure that content designers could use across different quest types while keeping requirements, rewards and validation clear for the teams responsible for implementing and testing the content.

Tournaments

I worked on the pipeline used to author tournaments across the live-service calendar, supporting daily, weekly and seasonal formats. The system needed to expose the rules and configuration required by content teams while remaining scalable enough to sustain repeated seasonal deliveries.

This work strengthened my approach to content architecture: translating a recurring player-facing feature into a reliable authoring structure that other teams could populate, test and maintain over time.

Event Pass

I contributed to the Event Pass system used to centralize seasonal rewards and connect live-service activity with the broader progression. The feature provided a clear structure for distributing rewards through event-linked progression and supported different configurations from season to season.

The design work required aligning reward pacing, player goals, progression state and the pass UI so the system felt connected to the rest of the game rather than like an isolated reward track.

Player Progression & Skill Tree

I was responsible for the golfer's level-progression structure and built the complete progression table and formulas used to balance XP, levels and long-term character growth. This work connected numerical progression curves to the pace at which players developed their golfer.

I also worked on the relationship between Skill Point investment, attributes and player abilities, aiming to ensure growth decisions translated into distinct builds rather than a simple linear accumulation of numerical upgrades.

Skills, Abilities & Ball Striking

I expanded the golfer ability system by designing new unlocks around the existing Skill Points framework, linking progression choices to player specialization.

Alongside progression, I designed gameplay and UI systems for ball striking, defining how new shot interactions worked in play and how their state, feedback and available options were communicated to the player. This connected systemic design directly with moment-to-moment gameplay and interface readability.
