Overview

Skull Towers was a tower-defense game in which the player had to stop enemy hordes before they reached their position. My work covered several parts of the upgrade: new core loops, a store system, progression and difficulty balance, a new reward system, and FX implemented directly in Unity.

My Contribution

Core Loop & Store

I designed new core loops aimed at improving retention and implemented them in code. I also created a new store system as part of the broader game upgrade.

Progression & Difficulty Balance

A major challenge was rebuilding the game progression around a new reward system. Weapons and towers had to unlock at appropriate points in the experience, while the equipment available before a level and the constructions available inside it had to remain consistent with the intended progression curve.

I balanced these elements by playing different levels and testing combinations of unlocked items, adjusting the progression so individual rewards, level difficulty and the broader game curve worked together.

Reward System

I balanced and implemented a new reward system tied to post-level chest unlocks. The rewards were organised into two groups: Main Rewards, which unlocked progression-critical content such as new arrows, bombs, bows and constructions; and Random Rewards, used once the available Main Rewards had been exhausted.

The weapons and towers offered through the reward screen were predetermined by the progression curve, allowing the reward flow to support the intended pace of player growth.

FX & Particle Systems

I designed and implemented several FX systems directly in Unity, using imported models from 3ds Max and textures from Photoshop where needed. The goal was to improve the final polish of the game, with effects covering elements such as enemy deaths, arrows and bombs.
