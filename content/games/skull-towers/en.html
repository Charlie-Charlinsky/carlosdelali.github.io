The Game

Skull Towers is a tower-defence game that combines strategy, RPG progression and first-person action. Players must protect a castle from waves of skeletons, building defences such as towers, catapults and cannons while taking a direct role in combat by firing a bow from the fortress’s defensive positions.

Progression broadens the player’s defensive options as they complete missions and unlock new weapons, arrows, bombs and structures. The resulting loop alternates between strategic preparation and direct execution: deciding how to strengthen the defence, entering combat, earning resources and using those rewards to prepare for the next encounter.

Contribution

Rewards & Progression System

My main contribution was designing, balancing and implementing a new reward system tied to the game’s progression. At the end of each level, players could open a number of chests based on the keys they had collected. The system tracked which major rewards were still pending and awarded new bows, arrows, bombs or structures in a controlled order aligned with the progression curve. Once all major rewards had been obtained, it switched to a random reward table containing currency, arrows or bombs.

Balancing & FX

To make the system work, I reviewed the overall progression and tested different unlock combinations, preventing rewards from appearing too early or undermining the difficulty of subsequent levels. Alongside the systems design and Unity coding, I also contributed to the visual polish by creating new particle and FX systems for elements such as enemy deaths, arrows and bombs.
