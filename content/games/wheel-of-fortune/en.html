Overview

Wheel of Fortune was a casino-game project developed at TWS Inventors of Play under that internal/project title. I was the design owner, responsible for defining the game concept, rules, behaviour documentation, mathematical requirements and implementation-facing specifications used by the production team.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and detailed behaviour specifications for the game, documenting the round flow, trigger conditions, reward resolution, state transitions, edge cases, UI feedback and presentation requirements. The documents served as the source of truth for the project while the mechanics were translated into production.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I developed the feature logic iteratively on paper and whiteboard, then worked with mathematicians to validate the probability and payout model. Unity/C# prototyping and simulation were used to test the behaviour and balance before final implementation decisions were made. I documented the feature states, trigger logic and reward resolution so the mathematical model and implementation could be evaluated against a single design reference.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked closely with mathematicians, engineers and artists throughout development, resolving questions between design intent, mathematical feasibility, technical implementation and visual presentation. Owning the design documentation allowed me to keep those disciplines aligned around one defined behaviour model.
