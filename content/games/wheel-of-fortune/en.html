The Game

Wheel of Fortune was conceived as a pocket-sized version of roulette, aimed primarily at mobile devices. The idea was to reduce the amount of information and decision-making present on a traditional roulette table until only a much more immediate system remained, while preserving the tension of choosing a number and waiting for the wheel to resolve.

Instead of using a conventional roulette wheel, the game presents a vertical wheel numbered from 0 to 9. Players can bet on any of those numbers and, when the prediction is correct, receive a base payout of 9 to 1. This structure reduces the game to a very simple interaction and allows each round to be understood and resolved within a few seconds.

An additional multiplier ranging from x1 to x4 is then applied to the result, creating a second layer of anticipation after the number has been hit. The result is a compact, fast casino experience particularly suited to short mobile sessions, with a publicly listed RTP of 97.47%.
