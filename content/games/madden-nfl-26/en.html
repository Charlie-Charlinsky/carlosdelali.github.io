Overview

For Madden NFL 26, my work centred on Superstar systems and live-service content. I designed Sphere of Influence, an RPG-inspired relationship and progression layer, and later authored Showdown live-service events built around distinct gameplay hooks, rules, objectives and rewards.

Across both areas, the common design problem was turning player actions into readable systemic consequences: relationships needed to react to choices and gameplay, while live events needed to transform a theme or football idea into a complete, testable ruleset.

My Contribution

Sphere of Influence

I designed Sphere of Influence as a relationship and progression system inside Superstar. The feature connected what the player did on and off the field with the characters around them, creating progression through influence, relationship levels and rewards.

The system was structured around a clear causal loop: player actions and choices affected characters; those relationships changed through positive or negative influence; progression through those relationships then altered the rewards, opportunities and consequences available to the player.

Relationship Progression & Rewards

My focus was on making relationships legible as a system rather than treating them as isolated narrative moments. Character progression, reward paths and gameplay-linked actions had to reinforce one another so the player could understand why a relationship changed and what that change meant for their Superstar career.

This required designing rules, progression structures and content relationships that could be authored at scale while remaining readable in UI and maintainable through data-driven pipelines.

Live Services — Showdown Events

Within Madden NFL 26 Live Services, I designed and authored Showdown events released across the year. Each event started from a gameplay hook or football idea and had to be translated into house rules, objectives, rewards and pacing that remained competitive and readable to players.

Beyond shipping the recurring event schedule, I also developed new event proposals and gameplay variations intended to expand the existing Showdown formula and keep the mode fresh throughout the live-service cycle.

Validation & Cross-Functional Delivery

The work depended on continuous alignment with engineering, production and QV. Rules and progression logic had to be implemented through data and content pipelines, then playtested and tuned against edge cases, technical constraints and the intended player experience.

This made Madden NFL 26 a strong example of my systems approach at AAA scale: define the loop, expose the rules clearly, connect the dependencies and iterate until the feature can be shipped and sustained.
