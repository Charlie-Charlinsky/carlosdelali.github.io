The Game

A Night with Cleo started from a simple and deliberately playful idea: make the player feel as if they were going on a date with Cleopatra. The goal was to use the slot mechanics themselves to make that encounter progress and become increasingly intimate, so Cleopatra would not simply act as decoration but as part of the reward itself.

The game uses a 5×3 layout with 20 paylines. Cleopatra acts as the Wild and the Key of Life works as the Scatter. Landing three or more Scatters triggers 15 Free Spins and moves the setting into a more private chamber, reinforcing the feeling that the date has progressed. Every win during the feature receives a x3 multiplier.

After a base-game win, players can also voluntarily enter a Gamble / Double-Up mechanic. Cleopatra presents two choices, and each correct selection doubles the prize while advancing the visual sequence of the encounter, for up to five consecutive rounds. A random Progressive Jackpot completes the system, connecting narrative, risk and reward within the same experience.

Main Features

5×3 Reels · 20 Paylines · Cleopatra Wild · Key of Life Scatter · 15 Free Spins · x3 Free Spins Multiplier · Gamble / Double-Up · Up to 5 Gamble Stages · Progressive Jackpot
