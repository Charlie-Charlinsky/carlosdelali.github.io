Overview

A Night with Cleo was an Egyptian-themed 5×3 slot with 20 paylines, built around Cleopatra Wild behaviour, Scatter-triggered free spins, a win multiplier, a player-controlled Gamble / Double-Up feature and a progressive-jackpot layer. At TWS Inventors of Play, I was the design owner for the game and carried its behaviour from documentation and feature definition through mathematical validation, prototyping and implementation support.

My Contribution

Design Ownership & Documentation

I created the GDDs and behaviour specifications used by the team, defining the core loop, symbol rules, feature triggers, state transitions, reward behaviour, edge cases and implementation-facing requirements. The documentation established how Cleopatra's Wild behaviour, the Key of Life Scatter, free spins, the Gamble / Double-Up sequence and jackpot presentation had to interact as one coherent game flow.

Feature Design & Mathematical Validation

I explored the feature behaviour on paper and whiteboard and worked closely with mathematicians to validate the probability model and risk/reward profile. Unity/C# prototypes and simulations supported iteration on trigger frequencies, payouts and balance. A central design challenge was connecting 15 Free Spins with an x3 win multiplier to the optional Gamble / Double-Up flow, where successful choices could repeatedly double a win through a staged reveal sequence.

Cross-Disciplinary Development

I worked alongside mathematicians, engineers and artists to translate the GDD into the final playable behaviour. We iterated across mathematics, implementation and presentation so the base slot, free-spin loop, gamble sequence and jackpot feedback remained understandable and consistent while preserving the intended pacing and risk profile.
