The Game

The Little Prince - Bubble Pop Journey is an F2P bubble shooter based on the film adaptation of The Little Prince. Its core mechanic involves shooting bubbles and matching three or more of the same colour to clear them, using wall rebounds, power-ups and different special bubble types to solve each level.

Progression takes players across a series of 3D maps inspired by the film, with more than 150 levels and objectives that evolve as the game advances. Some levels focus on clearing the board, occasionally within a time limit. Others require players to free specific elements or complete the objective within a limited number of shots, allowing a very simple rule set to generate a range of distinct puzzle scenarios.

Contribution

Level Design

My contribution focused on designing new levels and game modes for the project’s second phase. Each level began with a defined set of requirements: target difficulty, available colours, the special bubble types it had to feature—such as Unbreakable, Portals or Bouncy—and its assigned game mode, including Classic, Star or Time Attack. From this ruleset, I built the level layout and developed a solution that required players to make effective use of the available systems.

The process began on paper, sketching different sections of the level and thinking through how they might be solved before taking them into the engine. I then prototyped several versions directly in Unity and playtested them with the Lead Game Designer to evaluate game feel, difficulty and functionality. The version that best met the design goals became the final level, while discarded alternatives were retained as a reusable foundation for future content.
