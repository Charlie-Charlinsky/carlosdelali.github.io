Overview

The game was a bubble-pop experience built around several modes, including Time Attack, Puzzle and Classic.

My work focused on expanding the game with new levels and modes for its next episode. I took ideas from paper design into Unity prototypes, then validated them through hands-on playtesting.

My Contribution

Level Design

Each level started from a defined set of constraints: target difficulty, special bubble behaviours, bubble colour composition and the rules of its game mode. I sketched layouts on paper, worked through possible solutions and checked that the different gameplay elements interacted coherently.

I then prototyped the strongest candidates in Unity and played them with the Lead Game Designer. We iterated on game feel and functionality before selecting the final version. Discarded layouts also remained useful as a bank of ideas for future content.

Game Mode Design

For the next episode, I designed a Bubble Boss Fight mode. I first mapped its overall behaviour on paper, then analysed how the existing gameplay systems needed to interact so the mode worked as a coherent experience rather than a collection of disconnected mechanics.

Once the structure was stable, I prototyped the mode using the existing Unity bubble-pop engine and validated the full gameplay flow through playtesting.
