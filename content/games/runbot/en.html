The Game

Runbot is a 3D sci-fi endless runner in which players control a military prototype that has developed a mind of its own and is attempting to escape its creators. The character moves continuously through a futuristic city as the player jumps, slides, dodges obstacles and performs parkour moves to stay on the run for as long as possible.

The game combines touch controls with free-fall and flight sequences controlled through the device’s gyroscope. During each run, players collect batteries and resources to maintain their energy and upgrade different parts of the robot, including its armour, jetpack and arsenal. Variable route generation, progression and scoring make every attempt a new opportunity to travel farther and beat the player’s high score.

Contribution

Gameplay Upgrade & Implementation

At Genera, I worked on an existing version of Runbot to update its core loop and adapt the game for a new phase of the product. I began by assessing which mechanics should remain, which needed updating and how the new changes could be integrated without disrupting the pace of an endless runner built around quick reactions.

My contribution spanned several disciplines: I designed and implemented new features in Unity, rebalanced the game around the changes and worked on a range of technical and presentation elements, including shader adjustments, new FX and UI animations. The aim was to make every change directly testable in-game, assess its impact on the experience and continue iterating until the new elements worked together as a coherent system.
