Dream - Friday 17 July 2026

Part I

I dream that Eva and I have a child without even telling my mother or our families. We keep him hidden while taking care of him. The situation at this moment is strange; it seems that some kind of war and persecution is sweeping the country.

Part II

I am at home and a grass snake and a Cobra that my mother had bought, I do not know with what intention, enter the living room in Las Palas. I go to get tools to kill it. I demand that my mother come and help me kill it. She stops sewing to come and help me. The cobra gets under the sofa. Its colours change like a chameleon to a light blue with white spots. Suddenly it comes out from under the sofa and bites me on the instep of my left foot. Immediately afterwards I squeeze the wound to extract the venom.

Part III

I dream that I am in a hotel in Morocco where there seem to be ghosts. Someone knocks on the door and two frightened girls appear. They tell me that the receptionist wants to eat all of us. I start looking for a way to lock the door, but the locks are extremely strange and there are symbols on the lock, such as the icon of a cup. In the end nothing happens and my roommate does not suffer any attack either.

Part IV

As I leave the house, with the patio full of dry leaves and insects, I see a giant blowfly at the entrance. I kill it with poison. It drops dead.

Analysis

Central reading

Although the dream is divided into four parts, they all form a very coherent sequence: something new, intimate and valuable is being born in your life, but you feel that you must protect it from an unstable environment, family interference and invasive forces.

The recurring motif is the violated threshold:

The child remains hidden.

The cobra enters the house and hides under the sofa.

You try to close the hotel door.

The blowfly appears right at the entrance to the patio.

It is not, however, a dream of defeat. Only one threat manages to hurt you, and even then you extract the venom. No attack occurs in the hotel, and the blowfly is eliminated before it can enter. The progression moves from hiding and protecting, through being wounded, to detecting and destroying the threat at the threshold.

Part I: the hidden child during the war

In EGLDLS, birth does not usually announce a literal baby, but rather the appearance of something new on the horizon of life: a relationship, a home, a business, a project, an ideal or even a new state of consciousness. The newborn child represents the future, a force or creation that has just begun and still needs to develop.

The child that you and Eva have may represent:

A shared life project.

A new stage in your relationship.

A future identity that is still taking shape.

Something creative or professional that you consider a “child of the soul”.

Keeping him hidden even from your families indicates that this new reality is not yet ready to be subjected to outside opinions, expectations or interference. There is not necessarily guilt involved: it may be about protection and a need for autonomy.

War represents insecurity, fear of danger, inner conflict or the desire to free oneself from pressure that has already become difficult to bear. The child is therefore born in a hostile psychic context: there is a future, but also the feeling that this future must be preserved while you pass through a conflictive stage.

There is also a very interesting symbolic condensation: Eva appears first and then the snake. The book itself connects the snake with the story of Adam and Eve. This does not turn your partner into the threat; the dream may be superimposing the real Eva with the archetypal Eve in order to speak about creation, knowledge, temptation, birth and a change in consciousness.

Part II: the cobra brought into the house

The house symbolises the dreamer himself, his personality and his intimate life. The fact that the snake enters the living room places the conflict within the family, everyday and emotional sphere, rather than in some distant space.

The appearance of your mother is especially ambivalent. The book presents her as safety, shelter, warmth and protection, but also as possible oppression or an excessive prolongation of guardianship. In adults, her presence often points to a situation of great importance, uncertainty or anxiety.

Something very precise happens here:

In the first part you do not tell your mother about the birth.

In the second, your mother introduces —by buying it— a dangerous creature into the house.

When you demand her help, she stops sewing and comes with you.

Symbolically, this may express your fear that the family sphere, even without bad intentions, might introduce complications into a new life that you want to keep independent. But your mother does not appear as an enemy: when you need her, she participates in the defence.

The fact that she was sewing is significant. Sewing with a threaded needle represents improvement, reunion and consolidation of what was scattered or disordered. She abandons a slow, domestic repair in order to respond to an immediate danger.

The chameleonic cobra

In the book, the snake has a double symbolism: temptation, instinct and sexuality, but also wisdom, healing power and evolution. Venomous snakes, however, warn of fear of compromising situations, treacherous, dominant or implacable adversaries, and hidden matters that are difficult to control. The entry Viper, a close equivalent to the cobra, adds that it may represent a dangerous inner impulse that has not yet been integrated, or the fear of an external betrayal.

The colour change like a chameleon adds a feature that does not literally appear in the dictionary: the threat knows how to adapt, camouflage itself and present an appearance different from its true nature.

Light blue can symbolise truth, spirituality, purity and openness of consciousness, although in certain contexts it can also point to coldness, emptiness or danger. Bright white is associated with what is pure, new and trustworthy; but when covering a venomous snake, these colours may act as a mask: something dangerous presents itself under an innocent, elevated or reassuring appearance.

The bite on the left foot

The book interprets biting as attacking, assaulting or trying to take possession of something. Being bitten by an animal reflects the fear of becoming dominated by the instincts or passions represented by that animal.

The legs and feet symbolise how our affairs are “moving”, our ability to advance and the support upon which personality stands. The bite on the instep indicates that the threat does not destroy your mind or identity, but tries to affect your progress, stability and ability to stand firm.

The left foot can be secondarily associated with the receptive, intuitive and emotional side, although this precision is an extrapolation and not an explicit definition from the book.

The final reaction is favourable: you squeeze the wound to extract the venom. Wounds normally represent psychic wounds, especially to dignity, pride or sensitivity, and the book insists on discovering their cause and remedying it. That is exactly what you do: you do not wait passively; you locate the damage and try to expel what might spread.

Part III: the hotel in Morocco

The hotel represents a temporary situation, outside ordinary life. When the atmosphere is strange or unsettling, it reflects fear of circumstances completely different from those to which we are accustomed. Being abroad announces changes; if we remain there without moving on, it may reveal mental instability or difficulty controlling thoughts and concerns.

The ghosts never manifest directly. For that reason I would not literally apply the omen from the Ghost entry; they function more as an atmosphere of unease, like the feeling that invisible dangers or matters from the past could break in.

The receptionist should welcome and protect the guests, but supposedly wants to devour them. This is a complete reversal of her function: the figure responsible for receiving becomes a predatory figure. The Cannibal entry warns of self-interested people and also invites us to review what we use as mental or spiritual nourishment. In this context it may represent the fear that a person, institution or environment that should shelter you might instead try to take advantage of you or absorb you.

The door appears at critical moments or before a change. The lock represents a problem, dilemma or decision: opening it is equivalent to moving forward; closing it or being unable to open it constitutes a refusal or postponement. You try to close it, that is, to establish a boundary against intrusion, but the mechanism is incomprehensible to you.

The symbol of the cup on the lock is very significant. The cup represents the heart, love, life and destiny. It is as though protecting the room depended on understanding an emotional rule: trust, intimacy, bond or loyalty. Nevertheless, nobody enters and your roommate is not attacked either. The danger therefore remains a feared possibility rather than an accomplished harm.

Part IV: the giant blowfly

Dry leaves indicate wear, illness or problems; they can also represent something that has completed its cycle and lost its vitality. Insects reflect small annoyances, resentments, fears, contempt or fear of other people’s judgements and gossip. When they become gigantic, they may embody adversaries or authority figures who are feared and despised at the same time.

The blowfly corresponds to the Flies and mosquitoes entry. Flies represent annoying people or impulses and, in particular, the irritation produced by the excessive vanity of some people. Killing a fly expresses the desire to remove that influence from our life and prevent new problems.

What matters is that the blowfly is at the entrance and you manage to kill it. In the cobra scene, the danger entered the house and hurt you. Here you detect it at the threshold and eliminate it before it can enter. It is a clear image of learning: your defensive capacity is improving.

Combined interpretation

This dream seems to speak about the birth of a new stage that you want to build autonomously, probably related to Eva, your personal future or a life project that is deeply your own.

The main threat is not the destruction of that new reality, but the possibility that it may be contaminated by:

Family or external interference.

People who appear to have good intentions.

Inner conflicts, fears or impulses that make it difficult to move forward.

Environments that should protect you but that you perceive as absorbing or predatory.

The mother, the receptionist and the invasive animals form different versions of the same question: who may enter your intimate space, and how far do you allow them to intervene?

In a traditional or predictive reading, there is a warning about a changing, dominant or difficult-to-read situation or person who may present themselves under a reassuring appearance. But the overall ending does not announce defeat: you extract the venom, the hotel attack does not occur, and you eliminate the blowfly. It is a dream of warning, purification and strengthened boundaries.
